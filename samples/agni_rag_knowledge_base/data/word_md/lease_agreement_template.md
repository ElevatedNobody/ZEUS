# INDUSTRIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE FOR RAG)
**Document Type:** Lease Agreement  
**Classification:** Confidential – Legal  
**Entity:** ZEUS Manufacturing Ltd  
**Sample Lease ID:** LS-2024-1001  

## 1. Parties
**Lessor:** Skyline Realty LLP, registered office at Pune, Maharashtra.  
**Lessee:** ZEUS Manufacturing Ltd, CIN sample U21000MH2015PLC000001.

## 2. Demised Premises
Industrial shed admeasuring approximately 25,000 sq.ft. built-up at Plot 12, Industrial Area Phase 2, Pune, together with rights to common road access, parking for 20 four-wheelers, and proportionate undivided interest in amenities.

## 3. Term
Lock-in period of 24 months. Total term 60 months commencing 01-Apr-2024 and ending 31-Mar-2029 unless renewed.

## 4. Rent & Deposit
- Monthly rent: INR 2,50,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only) payable by the 7th of each month.
- Security deposit: INR 15,00,000 (interest-free, refundable within 45 days of handover subject to deductions for damages).
- Annual escalation: 7% on the rent then prevailing.

## 5. GST
Rent is subject to GST at applicable rates. Lessee shall pay GST in addition to base rent against valid tax invoice.

## 6. Use
Premises shall be used only for lawful industrial manufacturing, assembly, warehousing and ancillary office. No hazardous activity beyond consented limits. No sub-letting without prior written consent of Lessor.

## 7. Maintenance & Insurance
Lessee maintains interior; Lessor maintains structural members and common areas. Lessee shall keep comprehensive fire and burglary insurance naming Lessor as loss-payee for deposit amount.

## 8. Termination
Either party may terminate after lock-in with 90 days written notice. Breach of payment exceeding 30 days entitles Lessor to terminate after 15 days cure notice.

## 9. Handover
On expiry, Lessee shall return premises in broom-clean condition, remove branding, and settle all utility dues. Security deposit refund process starts after joint inspection.

## 10. Governing Law
Laws of India. Courts at Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
