# GST COMPLIANCE SOP – ZEUS (SAMPLE)
**Document ID:** SOP-TAX-GST-01  
**Effective:** 01-Apr-2025  

## Scope
Outward supplies, inward supplies, input tax credit (ITC), and periodic returns for ZEUS Manufacturing Ltd (GSTIN sample 27AABCU9603R1ZM).

## Monthly Calendar
- GSTR-1: file by 11th of following month
- GSTR-3B: file by 20th of following month
- Reconcile books vs GSTR-2B before ITC claim
- Vendor follow-up for missing invoices by 5th

## Responsibilities
- Finance Executive: data prep from ERP
- Tax Manager: review and submit
- CFO: oversight and escalation

## Document Retention
Returns, challans, working papers retained 6 years minimum in `tax/gst/` repository.

## Escalations
Any notice from tax department to be logged within 24 hours to Tax Manager and Legal.
