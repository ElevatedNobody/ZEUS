# MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT – KEY CLAUSES (SAMPLE)
**Contract Type:** MSA + SOW  
**Sample ID:** CNT-5001  

## Service Levels
Response: 4 business hours. Resolution: severity-based (S1 24h, S2 72h).

## Payment
Net 30 from invoice acceptance. Late fee not applicable if delay due to ZEUS approval.

## Confidentiality & Data
Vendor shall not process personal data outside agreed locations. Return/destroy data on termination.

## Indemnity & Liability
Vendor indemnifies IP infringement. Liability capped at 12 months fees except for fraud, bodily injury, or IP claims.

## Termination
Convenience: 60 days notice. For cause: 15 days cure period.
