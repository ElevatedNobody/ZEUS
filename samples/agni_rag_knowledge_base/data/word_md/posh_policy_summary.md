# POSH POLICY SUMMARY – ZEUS (SAMPLE)
ZEUS is committed to a workplace free from sexual harassment. Internal Committee (IC) is constituted as per the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013.

## Complaint
Written complaint to IC Presiding Officer within prescribed timelines. Interim relief may be recommended.

## Inquiry
IC completes inquiry within statutory timelines; report to employer; action as per law and service rules.

## Non-retaliation
Victimisation of complainant or witnesses is misconduct.

## Awareness
Annual training mandatory for all employees; attendance logged in LMS.
