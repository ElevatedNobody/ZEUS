# ZEUS EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK – KEY EXTRACTS (SAMPLE)
**Version:** 2025.2  
**Owner:** Human Resources  

## Employment Categories
Permanent, Fixed-term Contract, Trainee/Intern. Appointment letter states category, grade, location and CTC break-up.

## Working Hours
General shift 09:00–18:00 with 45 minutes break. Plant shifts as per shift roster. Biometric attendance mandatory at office and plant locations.

## Leave
- Casual Leave: 8 days / year
- Sick Leave: 8 days / year
- Earned Leave: 15 days / year (credit monthly)
- Maternity / Paternity: as per law
Carry-forward of EL capped at 45 days. Encashment on separation for eligible EL balance.

## Code of Conduct
Employees shall protect confidential information, avoid conflicts of interest, follow POSH policy, and not accept gifts above policy threshold without disclosure.

## Separation
Resignation notice: 30 days (L1–L2), 60 days (L3+), unless appointment letter states otherwise. Exit interview, asset return, and Full & Final settlement within 45 days of clearance.

## Documents in Employee Folder
KYC (PAN/Aadhaar masked copies), signed appointment letter, policy acknowledgements, increment letters, training records.
