# TDS DEDUCTION & DEPOSIT SOP (SAMPLE)
**Document ID:** SOP-TAX-TDS-01  

## Common Sections Used
- 192: Salary
- 194C: Contractual payments
- 194J: Professional fees
- 194I: Rent

## Process
1. Identify nature of payment at invoice booking.
2. Apply correct rate; lower rate only with valid Form 13 / certificate.
3. Deduct at the time of credit or payment, whichever is earlier.
4. Deposit challan by due date; file quarterly TDS returns.
5. Issue Form 16 / 16A to deductees.

## Controls
Maker-checker on challan preparation. Monthly exception report for non-deposited TDS.
