# EHS SAFETY BRIEF – PLANT OPERATIONS (SAMPLE)
## Golden Rules
1. Valid work permit for hot work, height, confined space.
2. PPE mandatory in shop floor: safety shoes, helmet, goggles as area-specific.
3. LOTO before maintenance on powered equipment.
4. Report near-miss within shift.

## Incident Response
First aid → inform shift in-charge → EHS → medical if required. Serious incidents escalate to Plant Head within 1 hour.

## Documents
Incident register, training matrix, equipment inspection checklists stored under `ehs/`.
